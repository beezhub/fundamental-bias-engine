and could not finish, not a reason the pair was rejected. `event:unchecked` on

fundamentals and nobody has looked at the news. `event:unknown` means a
calendar was consulted and could not answer, which is the one that asks the
reader to look at a calendar themselves within the hour. This column runs them
together; the shortlist below labels them apart.

`Agreement` is the share of pillar *weight* pointing the same way as the spread,
not the share of pillars. One heavy pillar dissenting moves it further than one
light pillar dissenting, which is the distinction a headcount would lose.
